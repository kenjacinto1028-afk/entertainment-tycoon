# Update 44 — multi-seed economic & continuity observations

**Build:** Update 44 / save v33. These are automated strategy samples and **do not prove that all player strategies are balanced**.

## Twelve-year stress by era and difficulty

- **9 distinct worlds**, each 12 years / 48 quarterly audits: **432 successful audits**, no illegal pre-debut productions or negative founder funding pool.
- **1,884 actual releases** across all tracked world companies; **3 rival bankruptcies**; **0 player furloughs** in these annually actively produced campaigns.
- **5.33 MB** largest 12-year serialized save.

## Matched-difficulty comparisons

- **18 worlds**: two identical seeds per era (Years 1, 4 and 25), each on casual, standard and challenging, **180 yearly audits**.
- Challenging finished below casual cash in **6/6** paired seeds; a lower starting cash is enforced, but long-run results remain stochastic and no strict outcome order is guaranteed.
- No magic competitor cash, infinite consumer demand or forced-market-share-balancing mechanism was introduced.

## Player-directed century continuity

- **2 × 100-year** weekly simulations, **200 yearly integrity audits**, 20 decade save/reloads, with a modest policy of one *affordably launched* original release per year. Decade checkpoints let the test recover after runtime limits; the program did not pretend the interrupted runs finished.
- Publishing: **2 valid appointments**, **99.962%** weeks with executive authority, **100** released works, max save **32.52 MB**.
- Games: **3 valid appointments**, **99.942%** authorized weeks, **100** released works, max save **15.55 MB**.
- No change in firm ID, founder shares or IP ownership was permitted by the succession action. Active management *can* continue lawfully for a century with adequate recurring sales, staff and player decisions; **this is not guaranteed** for bankrupt/passive companies.

## Calibration caveats

- Observed campaigns generated sizeable accumulated cash in some entertainment sectors, but comparable difficulty outcomes vary by seed. **No global revenue multiplier was changed without broader calibrated player-market evidence.**
- Testing currently covers compact-to-medium worlds rather than maximum-size browser performance; legal succession without candidates or cash still requires a real exit/new-company decision, never an automatic transfer.
- Furlough suspends production if payroll cannot be funded, leaving existing debt intact. It is an intentional modeled distress consequence, not a rescue.
